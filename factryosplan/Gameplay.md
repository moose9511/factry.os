## Overview
Factory/management game where you manage your factory and power management though a TUI like interface, all visuals look like a program run through a Linux terminal.
## Movement/Character
A main TUI visual panel that shows the scanned world. Start with 1 probe that you can control to collect material. You start with a pod that has power from it's solar panel.
## Visuals
TUI with multiple panels you can open. Hopefully get some graphs of certain metrics. Have a terminal that you can manually control certain things with.

## Mining
Probes can collect material. Drills collect material
## Logistics
Probes will manually carry material to and from places, use conveyer belts to lessen the distance probes carry things.
## Power
Create grids connecting and expanding your power grid. Use solar panels. Hydrothermic idk I'll think of some later.
## UI
UI can be upgraded for ease of use. Start with controlling everything at the terminal with commands, like telling your first probe where to go. Get software updates to increase interactability such as buttons for zooming in and out
